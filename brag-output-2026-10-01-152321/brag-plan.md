# FitGenie Hyper-Cinematic Launch Commercial

## Creative angle
**Move through your whole fitness day.** A premium camera journey starts inside the real FitGenie mark, dives into the real app surfaces, and ends with the product resolving into one confident brand lockup. The treatment uses dimensional phone frames, parallax, macro crops, perspective drift, tactile taps, and restrained light to make the UI feel physical without changing the UI itself.

## Guardrails
- Landscape, 1920x1080, exactly 20 seconds, 30 fps, H.264/AAC.
- App-store/cinematic tone: dark navy, FitGenie purple, teal and warm accents.
- Real assets: FitGenie logo, repository preview captures, `chest_1.gif`, bundled local music and UI SFX.
- No Flutter source changes, no fabricated claims, no invented statistics or capabilities.
